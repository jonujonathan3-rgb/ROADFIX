Road Problem Reporting

Please read the declaration and terms before continuing.

Declaration

I understand that the information provided in this road problem report should be accurate and related to an actual road or public safety issue.

I agree to provide correct information, photographs and location details wherever possible to help the concerned authorities identify and resolve the issue.

Conditions & Terms

  • I will provide accurate and truthful information about the road problem.
  • I will select the appropriate category for the reported road or safety issue.
  • I will provide the correct location or nearby landmark whenever possible.
  • I will upload clear photographs that represent the actual road condition, if photographs are available.
  • I will not intentionally submit false, misleading, abusive or duplicate reports.
  • The report may be reviewed and verified by the concerned authority before action is taken.
  • Submitting a report does not guarantee immediate repair or resolution.
  • ROADFIX may use submitted information to assess road safety and prioritize maintenance work.
  • Emergency situations requiring immediate assistance should be reported to the appropriate emergency services.
⚠️
Important: Please make sure the information you provide is genuine and related to a real road or public safety issue.
